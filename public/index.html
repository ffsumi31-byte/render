What happens next

The ApiDuck Windows/Linux launchers will be published with checksums in GitHub Releases after the live service is validated. The current source scripts still point to Netlify, not Render.

→
When released

Run the launcher in your project folder and enter your sk-gw-… key at its hidden prompt. Provider keys never go into the launcher.

Good to know

Which key do I enter?

Your individual sk-gw-… customer key. Provider credentials remain on the server.

Do token grants reset?

No. Lifetime grants do not expire or reset monthly.

Why can’t I connect yet?

We must validate the live database, provider access, and quota enforcement first. Publishing an untested launcher would be misleading.
